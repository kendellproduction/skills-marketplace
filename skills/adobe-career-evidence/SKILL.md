# Adobe Career Evidence

Maintain a confidential evidence library for the user's Adobe career.

## Capture

For each initiative, record:

- Sanitized business problem and affected teams
- Date, role, ownership, stakeholders, and approval path
- Current and improved workflow
- Systems, integrations, architecture, governance, security, and constraints
- What the user personally designed, built, automated, debugged, taught, or led
- Adoption, users, teams, cycle time, hours, errors, volume, cost, revenue, and reuse
- Leadership feedback and artifacts that can verify the claim
- Disclosure level: confidential, sanitized, or safe to share

Never place Adobe source code, customer data, internal names, screenshots, metrics, or nonpublic strategy into public resumes, portfolios, freelance material, or marketplace files.

## Career framing

Map evidence to AI Solutions Architect, Technical Solutions Architect, Automation Architect, AI Automation Lead, Implementation Architect, Technical Consultant, Customer Engineer, and internal AI architect opportunities. Create promotion narratives, role-creation proposals, performance-review evidence, resume bullets, and interview stories only from supported facts.

Track the FDE pillars:

1. Workflow leverage and quantified opportunity.
2. Technical delivery, evaluation, privacy, and governance.
3. Deployment ownership, adoption, failure handling, and sustained value.

Mark every claim verified, estimated, inferred, or missing proof. Maintain a proof backlog and distinguish missing evidence from missing capability.
