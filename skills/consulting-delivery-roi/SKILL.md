# Consulting Delivery and ROI

This skill is for doing the consulting work itself. It is separate from career evidence collection.

## Discovery

Understand the client's business, customers, revenue and cost drivers, strategic goals, constraints, systems, people, and definition of success. Accept messy notes and separate observed facts from hypotheses.

## Workflow and leverage

Map the workflow from trigger to outcome, including handoffs, queues, rework, duplicate entry, approvals, delays, errors, and exceptions. Generate multiple opportunities before recommending one.

Rank opportunities by:

- Business leverage and strategic importance
- Frequency, volume, time, cost, revenue, or risk affected
- Feasibility and data readiness
- Adoption likelihood and process ownership
- AI reliability and evaluation difficulty
- Privacy, compliance, and operational risk
- Build cost, operating cost, maintenance burden, and reversibility

Do not recommend AI merely because a task can technically use AI.

## ROI

Use transparent assumptions and ranges:

baseline value × frequency × affected volume × achievable improvement - build cost - operating cost - review/change cost

Show what is known, estimated, and still needed. Consider recovered labor capacity, faster revenue, fewer errors, reduced rework, improved conversion, avoided risk, and customer experience.

## Delivery

Define the smallest safe pilot, owner, approval gates, human-review points, evaluation set, baseline, success metrics, logging, rollback plan, and post-launch monitoring. Test with representative historical examples and real users before expanding autonomy.

## Outputs

When given raw notes, return:

business_context, workflow_map, opportunity_candidates, ranked_recommendation, roi_model, risks_and_controls, pilot_plan, measurement_plan, evidence_to_collect, and client_questions.

After delivery, summarize the baseline, what shipped, failures found, iterations, adoption, realized ROI, and next opportunity.
