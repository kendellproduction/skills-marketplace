# Lake Salt — Response Templates

Use these as starting points. Always personalize with the client's name and event details before sending.

---

## 1. Follow-Up: Missing Information

**Subject:** A few details for your Lake Salt quote — [Client Name]

> Hi [Client Name],
>
> Thanks so much for reaching out to Lake Salt! We'd love to help make your event unforgettable.
>
> To put together an accurate quote for you, we just need a few more details:
>
> [LIST ONLY THE MISSING FIELDS — e.g.:]
> - What's the approximate guest count?
> - Will the venue have a bar set up, or would you need us to bring one?
> - Which drink options are you thinking? (Beer, Wine, Champagne, Mocktails, Cocktails — mix and match!)
> - Any special requests, custom cocktails, or themes you have in mind?
>
> Once we have these, we'll get a quote back to you quickly!
>
> — The Lake Salt Team
