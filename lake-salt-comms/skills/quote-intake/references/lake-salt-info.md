## What We Do
Lake Salt is a mobile bartending and events service. We bring the bar to you — complete with professional bartenders, setup, and breakdown.

## Services
- Full mobile bar setup (we bring the bar if the venue doesn't have one)
- Professional bartending staff
- Custom cocktail menus
- Beer, wine, champagne, mocktails, and cocktail service
- Event consultation on drink packages

## Drink Packages Available
- **Beer** — domestic and/or craft selection
- **Wine** — red, white, rosé
- **Champagne** — for toasts or full service
- **Mocktails** — non-alcoholic specialty drinks
- **Cocktails** — classic and custom craft cocktails
- Packages can be mixed and matched to fit the event

## Event Types We Service
- Weddings
- Corporate events
- Private parties
- Birthday celebrations
- Baby/bridal showers
- Holiday parties
- Outdoor events

## How It Works
1. Client inquires and provides event details
2. Lake Salt provides a custom quote
3. Client confirms and books
4. Lake Salt arrives, sets up, services the event, and breaks down

## What We Need from the Venue
- Access for setup (typically 1–2 hours before start)
- Power access if needed for certain setups
- If venue has a bar: we work with it; if not: we bring ours
